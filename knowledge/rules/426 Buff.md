# 426. Buff

> [!NOTE] Fuente oficial
> Sección extraída de `docs/core_rules.md`.

**426.** Counter

**426.1.** Countering is the act of negating the execution, activation, or otherwise playing of a card or ability by
a player.

**426.1.a.** A card or ability that is Countered does nothing and is cleared from the chain.

**426.1.a.1.** Cards that are cleared from the chain in this way are placed in the trash.

**426.1.b.** A card that is Countered is not considered to have been played for abilities that trigger on
cards being played.

**426.1.c.** Countering does not refund any costs paid to play a card, activate an ability, or trigger an
ability.

**426.1.c.1.** This includes additional costs.
**426.2.** Countering is a Limited Action.

**426.2.a.** Players may only Counter cards and abilities when directed to by Game Effects.

**426.3.** This action is formatted as "Counter [a card or ability on the chain]."
