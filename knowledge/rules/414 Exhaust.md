# 414. Exhaust

> [!NOTE] Fuente oficial
> Sección extraída de `docs/core_rules.md`.

**414.** Draw

**414.1.** Drawing a card takes a single card from a zone and adds it to the player's Hand.

**414.1.a.** Unless specified otherwise, drawing takes cards from the top of the Main Deck.

**414.2.** This is a Limited Action.

**414.2.a.** Each player draws 1 during the Draw Phase on their turn.

**414.2.b.** The player may draw cards when instructed to do so by other game effects.

**414.3.** This action, when instructed, is formatted as "Draw X."

**414.4.** If a player attempts to draw more cards than are available in their Main Deck, they do the following:

**414.4.a.** Draw as many as possible.

**414.4.b.** Perform a Burn Out.
See rule 432. Burn Out for more information.

**414.4.c.** Draw the remaining cards needed to complete the Draw action.
