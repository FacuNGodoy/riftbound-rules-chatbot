# 419. Play

> [!NOTE] Fuente oficial
> Sección extraída de `docs/core_rules.md`.

**419.** Heal

**419.1.** Damage being cleared from Units is Healing.

**419.1.a.** If Damage is cleared for any reason it is considered Healing.

**419.2.** More than one Unit can be Healed at the same time.

**419.3.** Healing is a Limited Action.

**419.3.a.** Players may only Heal units when game effects instruct them to, or at specific points in the
turn.
